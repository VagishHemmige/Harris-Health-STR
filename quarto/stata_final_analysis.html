Author

Vagish Hemmige

The do-file behind the published paper, in its natural sections. It starts from the “Data Analytic file” made by Step 8. It was run by hand a section at a time, so it has a stop line partway down (now a comment) and creates VirologicallySuppressed2 twice; read it as a record, not a script that runs straight through. Every section links to the R steps that replace it, in steps 6 to 9, and every R step links back here.
