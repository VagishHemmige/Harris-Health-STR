Published results and the code behind them

Author

Vagish Hemmige

ResultsWhere these numbers come from

Every number on this page is quoted from the published article (Hemmige et al. 2018), with its place there. None was produced by the code in this repository, which has not been run on the study data. The article’s tables are not reproduced; read them in the article (free in PubMed Central).

This page is a map from each reported result to the step of the code that computes it, so that a reader can see what each number is and an authorized analyst can check the R version against the article. “Final Analysis.do” is the published analysis; the R steps translate it.

The cohort

Result (article, Results, first two paragraphs) Computed by
2,095 patients entered care in the period; 1,028 met the inclusion criteria apply-exclusions (cohort_flow gives the patients left after each exclusion). The 2,095 is not in the code.
STR more likely if male, younger, lower baseline viral load, higher baseline CD4, not injecting drugs test-baseline-differences
After weighting, the STR and MTR groups were much more similar check-weighted-balance
622 (60.5%) STR patients, all on tenofovir/emtricitabine/efavirenz define-exposure-and-time (str); counts of initial_regimen in patients
410 (39.5%) MTR patients: 65.2% atazanavir/ritonavir and 34.8% darunavir/ritonavir, each with tenofovir/emtricitabine The same

The Results give 410 MTR patients and the abstract 406; 622 + 406 = 1,028, the cohort size the article reports. Both are quoted as the article has them.

Retention and viral suppression

Result (article, Results, third paragraph) Computed by
Retention in care: 80.7% STR vs 72.7% MTR; OR 1.57 (95% CI 1.17-2.11), p = 0.003; adjusted OR 1.49 (1.10-2.02), p = 0.011 define-retention (retained_year_one); fit-outcome-models
Suppression at any point in year one: 84.4% vs 77.6%; OR 1.56 (1.14-2.15), p = 0.006; adjusted OR 1.41 (1.02-1.96), p = 0.04 define-patient-outcomes (vl_suppressed_year_one); fit-outcome-models. The code, the record of what was done, counts a viral load of 400 or less; the article’s text says below 400.
Suppression on the original regimen: 79.4% vs 72.9%; OR 1.43 (1.07-1.92), p = 0.016; adjusted OR 1.28 (0.95-1.73), p = 0.11 vl_suppressed_on_initial_regimen, the same two steps, in days 0-364. The saved Stata looked at all follow-up (why the R differs).

Adherence

Result (article, Results, fourth paragraph) Computed by
At least 80% of days covered for the year: 33.0% STR vs 30.1% MTR; OR 1.14 (0.87-1.50), p = 0.328; adjusted OR 1.04 (0.79-1.38), p = 0.77 adherent_pdc_80 in define-patient-outcomes; fit-outcome-models, over days 0-364. The saved Stata averaged over all follow-up (why the R differs).
At least 80% of days covered on the initial regimen: 29.6% vs 25.9%; OR 1.20 (0.91-1.60), p = 0.195; adjusted OR 1.08 (0.81-1.45), p = 0.586 adherent_pdc_80_initial, the same two steps, over days 0-364
Suppressed despite no fill in the previous 90 days: 13.3% MTR vs 15.8% STR, p = 0.28 flag-possible-outside-pharmacy (possible_outside_pharmacy, outside_pharmacy_test)
Retention and suppression rerun without those patients: similar results (data not shown) exclude-possible-outside-pharmacy. The article’s models were run in a scratch file not kept here.

The abstract calls the adherence measure a medication possession ratio; the Methods and Results call it the proportion of days covered, which is what the code computes.

Sensitivity analyses

Result (article, Results, fifth paragraph, and Discussion) Computed by
Fewer patients with psychiatry visits received an STR: 22.5% vs 28.3%, p = 0.035 psych_test in add-psych-visits-to-propensity
Adding psychiatry visits to the propensity score did not change the odds ratios meaningfully (data not shown) psych_models, the same step. Final Analysis.do fitted these models with the original weights; the R uses the new ones.
Year by year, with 400 copies/mL for 2008 and 50 afterwards: suppression lower, STR advantage unchanged (data not shown) suppression-threshold-by-year, in days 0-364
Separate analyses for men and women: little change (Discussion) Not in the kept code; run in a scratch file not kept here

Regimen switches

Result (article, Results, last paragraph) Computed by
Switched within the first year: 53 MTR (13.1%) vs 97 STR (15.6%), p = 0.26 changed_regimen in define-patient-outcomes, in days 0-364 (Final Analysis.do counted a switch at any time)
Suppressed at the time of switch: 22 of 53 MTR (41.5%) vs 34 of 97 STR (35.1%), p = 0.43 suppression-at-switch, for switches in year one
Median days to switch: 162 (IQR 90-244) STR vs 140 (42-214) MTR, p = 0.17 Not in the kept code
3 STR patients switched to another STR; 9 MTR patients switched to an STR Not in the kept code

The rows marked “not in the kept code” came from do-files that are not part of this repository, because they did not feed the published analysis.
